# Dictation acceptance for ThumbFree.exe --self-test on lab.html (see run.ps1): every box on the page, in Tab order.
# Stops at the first failed check, so nothing is ever typed into the wrong window.
stoponfail
wait 1000
expecttitle: Dictation Lab
expectfocus: Message
field
expectfield: multi-line
clipboard
ptt: Hello comma this is a test full stop
expect: Hello, this is a test.
# A space joins it to the text already in the box.
ptt: Second sentence.
expect: test. Second sentence.
# Alt+Shift+Z types the last dictation again.
again
expect: Second sentence. Second sentence.
# Live typing: each phrase is typed once the next one has started, the last when the dictation ends.
live: We shipped the build. | Then we tested it. | It works.
expect: Second sentence. We shipped the build. Then we tested it. It works.
# A correction across pauses: nothing is typed until its new value has come.
live: Meet me at the cafe at 6, | sorry, | 7 tonight.
expect: It works. Meet me at the cafe at 7 tonight.
# A list said in phrases waits, then goes on lines of its own.
live: Things to do today: | order groceries, | go on a walk, | and call mom.
expect: tonight.\nThings to do today:\n- Order groceries.\n- Go on a walk.\n- Call mom.
clipboard
